# Hiatme Tool Suite 4.0.0.86

A billing pill in the title bar shows how many days in the past three months still have trips ready to bill. Click it for the days and trip counts. Click a day to open Billing and load that day. Nothing is submitted until you click Submit.

Circles mark what needs attention. A free driver gets a star. Billable days get a red dollar sign, and that one stays up on every tab. A driver whose day is over but who still has an open ticket gets a skull. Click the thing a circle is pointing at and it puffs into smoke.
