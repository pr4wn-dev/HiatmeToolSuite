What's new in 4.0.0.66:

Popups now stick to the schedule you have open
- Trip edits on a different day no longer pop up. Watching Remie shuffle the 18th
  while you are building the 17th was most of the noise, and it was the one thing
  you could not act on from where you were sitting
- Saves and someone opening or closing a day still come through from any day, in
  quieter wording, since those are a handful a day and tell you when a schedule is
  finished or when someone else is about to be in it
- Your own schedule is unchanged, including the sticky warning when a teammate
  saves a newer copy of the day you are editing
- Nothing is hidden: the history drawer still lists every day in full
