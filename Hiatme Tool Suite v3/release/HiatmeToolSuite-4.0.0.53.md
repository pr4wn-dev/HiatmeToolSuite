What's new in 4.0.0.53:

Live schedule activity
- Bottom-right toasts show what other desks are doing in the Schedule Builder as it happens: moved, pasted, Suggest, cut, delete, Reroutes, Cancels, notes, undo/redo, saved
- Hollow dot = that desk has not saved yet; fills when their save lands
- "Saved rev N" toast when you are on an older revision of the same day; click it to LOAD
- "Save rejected" toast when someone published first; click it to LOAD, then redo your change
- Toolbar pill shows who else has the day open and how many unsaved edits they have
- Click any toast or the pill for the full history of the day
