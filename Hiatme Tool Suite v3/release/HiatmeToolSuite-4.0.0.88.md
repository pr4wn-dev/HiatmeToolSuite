# Hiatme Tool Suite 4.0.0.88

Driver tabs stay put when you open a driver's schedule. A free driver shows a star on the tab while that tab is on screen, and a circle tracks the tab only while it is off screen. The star stays until the driver is no longer free. The chime plays when a driver first becomes free, not every time the circle appears. Clicking a tab does not puff smoke.

Billable days use a green dollar sign. A driver whose day is over but who still has an open ticket gets a skull.
